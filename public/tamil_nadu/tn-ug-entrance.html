Tamil Nadu UG Admissions

Official Counselling Portals Search — 2026-27

Portal Name Stream Authority Courses Description
TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) Engineering Directorate of Technical Education, Anna University B.E., B.Tech, Integrated M.Tech (CSE) Single-window counselling based on Class 12 marks. Registration usually opens in May.
TNGASA (Tamil Nadu Govt Arts & Science Admissions) Arts & Science Directorate of Collegiate Education B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Lit. (Tamil) Unified portal for UG seats in govt arts/science colleges. Applications typically open in early May.
TN Medical Selection Medical & Health Directorate of Medical Education & Research MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, Allied Health Sciences NEET-UG based counselling. Registration opens after NEET-UG results, usually late June.
TNDALU (Tamil Nadu Dr. Ambedkar Law University) Law State law university, affiliating all TN law colleges B.A. LL.B., B.B.A. LL.B., B.Com. LL.B., LL.B. Admissions to 5-year and 3-year LLB programmes. 5-year applications generally open by April–May.
TNAU (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University) Agriculture Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, Coimbatore B.Sc. (Agriculture), B.Sc. (Horticulture), B.Tech (Food Tech.), B.Sc. (Forestry) UG admissions across constituent and affiliated colleges based on Class 12 marks.
TANUVAS (Tamil Nadu Veterinary and Animal Sciences University) Veterinary & Fisheries Tamil Nadu Veterinary & Animal Sciences University, Chennai B.V.Sc. & A.H., B.F.Sc., B.Tech (Food Tech.) Counselling follows state merit list and reservation rules.
TNPESU (Tamil Nadu Physical Education and Sports University) Sports TN Physical Education & Sports University, Chennai B.P.Ed., B.Sc. (Physical Education), B.Sc. (Yoga) Admission based on merit and sporting record.
